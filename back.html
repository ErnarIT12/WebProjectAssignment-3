BACK EXERCISES

Exercises for a stronger and wider back.

Pull-Ups

A bodyweight exercise that targets the upper back and arms.

Technique:

  • Grip the bar firmly.
  • Pull your body upward.
  • Lower yourself slowly.

T-bar Row

A compound exercise for building back strength.

Technique:

  • Keep your back straight.
  • Pull the bar toward your body.
  • Lower the bar with control.

Lat Pulldown

Targets the latissimus dorsi and upper back.

Technique:

  • Grip the bar slightly wider.
  • Pull it toward your chest.
  • Return slowly.
