CROSSFIT EXERCISES

High-intensity movements for strength and endurance.

Burpees

A full-body exercise that increases endurance.

Technique:

  • Start standing.
  • Move into a plank position.
  • Jump back up.

Box Jumps

A plyometric exercise for explosive leg power.

Technique:

  • Stand in front of the box.
  • Jump onto the box.
  • Step down carefully.

Kettlebell Swing

Develops hip power, strength and endurance.

Technique:

  • Hold the kettlebell with both hands.
  • Drive the hips forward.
  • Control the kettlebell downward.
