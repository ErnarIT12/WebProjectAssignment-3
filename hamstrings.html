HAMSTRINGS & GLUTES

Train the back of your legs and glutes.

Romanian Deadlift

Targets the hamstrings, glutes and lower back.

Technique:

  • Keep your back straight.
  • Move your hips backward.
  • Return to the starting position.

Leg Curl

An isolation exercise for the hamstrings.

Technique:

  • Position your legs correctly.
  • Curl your heels upward.
  • Return slowly.

Hip Thrust

A popular exercise for the glutes.

Technique:

  • Place your upper back on a bench.
  • Drive your hips upward.
  • Squeeze the glutes at the top.
