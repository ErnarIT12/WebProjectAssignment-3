ARM EXERCISES

Train your biceps and triceps.

Barbell Curl

A classic exercise for building the biceps.

Technique:

  • Hold the bar with both hands.
  • Curl the bar upward.
  • Lower it slowly.

Hammer Curl

Works the biceps and forearm muscles.

Technique:

  • Keep palms facing each other.
  • Curl the dumbbells upward.
  • Lower with control.

Triceps Pushdown

An effective cable exercise for the triceps.

Technique:

  • Hold the cable handle.
  • Push it down.
  • Return slowly.
