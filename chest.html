CHEST EXERCISES

Three basic exercises for chest.

Bench Press

A basic exercise for buildingchest strength.

Technique:

  • Lie on the bench.
  • Grip the bar firmly.
  • Lower and press the bar.

Incline Dumbbell Press

Targets the upper part of the chest.

Technique:

  • Set the bench at an angle.
  • Hold dumbbells at chest level.
  • Press them upward.

Push-Ups

A simple bodyweight chest exercise.

Technique:

  • Keep your body straight.
  • Lower your chest.
  • Push yourself upward.
